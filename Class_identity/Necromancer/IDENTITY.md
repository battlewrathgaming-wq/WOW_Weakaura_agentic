# Necromancer — Identity

_The imagined feel of Conquest of Azeroth's Necromancer — read through the Forsaken,
Animation-spec lens (Battlewrath's own character). This is **feeling, not mechanics**: no claim
here is about how the class works, only about what it *is*. The rule-layer belongs to
[Class_design/Necromancer](../../Class_design/Necromancer/FINDINGS.md). Consumers read this and
riff — inspiration, not contract._

## In one breath

A **victim who became a master.** Undeath was done to her, by force, in the fall of Lordaeron.
She broke the chains — and then took up the very art that unmade her. She does not fear death;
death already happened. What is left of her is cold, and it obeys.

## Archetype — the freed thrall who took up the whip

She is not the willing cultist who *aspired* to undeath as an honor. She was the raised corpse,
the commanded thing — and she **turned the instrument of her violation into her mastery.** So
when she raises the dead now, it is not from a living lord's contempt; it is from *inside*
death, with a terrible intimacy. She commands a whole necropolis — skeletal archers, ghouls,
abominations, tomb kings, plaguefathers — because she was once the thing that marched at
another's command. And her power is not even her own: it **rises from the mass of the dead she
carries.** She houses them within her own capacity, a reliquary of servants; her strength is
the weight of the graves she keeps.

## Register — cold, defiant, spent of grief

The horror is over. There is no terror left to feel, no bargain left to regret — the cost was
paid, against her will, long ago. What remains is **control, ownership, grim purpose.** She is
not a hero; nothing here is redeemed. She is one dark figure in a roster of dark things, and
her only claim to sympathy is that she *owns herself* — self-authorship turned against the
power that once wrote her. She persists. She is one of the already-dead who simply **refused to
stop.**

## Narrative — a discipline forged from atrocity, and the fallout after

The Scourge's rise was so brutal it cracked dark magic open and **formalized it into a field of
study** — Scholomance beneath Caer Darrow, the Cult of the Damned, the Thuzadin. The
Necromancer is a **scholar of that curriculum** — meticulous, iron-willed, clinical — and for a
Forsaken, mastering it means *taking the enemy's own knowledge and making it hers.* (Conquest
names this plainly: her arts are literally *Scourge Disciple*; her escape a *Scourge
Transporter*.) She lives now in the **fallout** — the wreckage of the invasion, the poisoned
Plaguelands, the Forsaken's hard-won and precarious independence. She sustains her own undeath
by **leeching life through the dead she commands**, holding herself together, day after day, in
a world that already ended.

## Sensory palette — the corner Suno pulls hardest

- **Materials:** bone, grave-dirt, rusted iron, rotting cloth, crypt-stone. Morion-black and
  grave-grey.
- **Color & light:** plague-green witchfire, lich-blue frost, shadow — sickly luminescence in
  the dark.
- **Sound:** a low **dirge**; bone-rattle and the drag of shuffling feet; the wet cough of
  plague; the crack of frost (*Lichfrost*, *Glacial Tap*); a whispered command the dead obey.
- **Motion:** the slow, unstoppable **march of the dead**; the patient shamble, then the sudden
  swarm.
- **Emblem:** a raven perched on a triangle — *"death, and the renewal found in death."*
  Renewal *is* the Forsaken's whole story.

## For the puller (mood seed)

Cold. Funereal, but *marching* — a dirge with a war-drum beneath it. Grand and grim, not
frantic; the confidence of something that cannot be killed again. Low choir, deep percussion,
iron and bone; green-lit and frost-bitten. Not evil-triumphant — **evil-enduring.** A dead
sorceress conducting an army of the dead, and holding herself together with the same hand.

## Unexplored (the honest rim)

Read through one lens: **Forsaken + Animation.** Voiced through one character — Battlewrath's,
a she — while the class itself is player-agnostic; **her** softer edges are a *character* matter,
out of scope for the class's identity. Not yet imagined: the other spec registers (**Death**,
the plague/disease branch; **Rime**, the frost/lich-cold branch) and other race-origins (a
living human Scholomance student, an unrepentant Scourge loyalist), each of which would shade
this differently. Named here so the gaps are loud, not papered over.
