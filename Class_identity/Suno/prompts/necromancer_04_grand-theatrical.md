## Lyrics (Write box) — commanding line structure; banshee at the crescendo

```
[Intro]
(a low pipe organ and a distant war-drum, strings gathering — the calm before the rise)

[Verse]
(low, cold, commanding)
Once, a voice commanded me.
Rise.
And I rose.
Now the voice is mine.

[Build-Up]
(power chords and orchestra swelling, double-kick entering, the choir gathering)
(rising)
Every stone, I keep.
Every bone, I own.
The dead are the weight that makes me strong.

[Chorus]
(huge — operatic lead soaring over sharp power chords, gang-choir behind)
Rise.
Rise of the grave —
answer the hand that will not be denied.
(gang-choir)
We are the risen. we are the tide.
(lead, soaring)
And I am the will they cannot survive.

[Verse]
(cold, certain)
I do not beg the dark.
I command it.
What death took from me,
I take back — and keep.

[Chorus]
(bigger)
Rise.
Rise of the grave —
answer the hand that will not be denied.
We are the risen. we are the tide.
And I am the will they cannot survive.

[Breakdown]
(strip to organ and a single voice — the moment of command)
(low, whispered, then swelling)
Come forth.
...come forth...

[Crescendo]
(full band and choir erupt, power chords driving, a banshee wail tearing over the top)
(belted, rising into a banshee wail)
(wailing)
Rise — and do not stop.

[Final Chorus]
(everything — the army risen, the peak)
Rise of the grave —
answer the hand that will not be denied.
We are the risen. we are the tide.
And I am the will they cannot survive.

[Outro]
(the march of the risen receding — organ, drum, a last low chord)
(cold, certain)
Rise.
And follow me.
```
