# Prompt — Necromancer × Form 02 · Driver B · **v3 (line-structure + terminology)**

_Builds on [v2](necromancer_02_funeral-march_v2.md) (banshee cues kept). **Two changes, from Battlewrath's
v2 grade:**_
1. _**Line-structure test** — the opening sentence is broken into three hushed lines. **Hypothesis
   (Battlewrath):** line structure *commands the word* (delivery/phrasing) where meta-tags only forecast.
   Observed in v2: "one light" **folded/collapsed** in delivery; breaking it onto its own small line should
   make that deliberate. **This run tests it.**_
2. _**Terminology** — grace matching what the song carries: **going → fading** (the image); **"I do not
   stop" → "I am eternal"** ("stop" lands blunt; "eternal" keeps the enduring meaning with grace — and reads
   closer to the identity: evil-enduring)._

## Lyrics (Write box) — v3

```
[Intro]
(instrumental: a single deep funeral drum, a tolling bell, a low drone rising)

[Verse]
(whispered, spent, breath running thin)
Cold in the long dark.
one light.
And it is fading.
I have paid. I have paid — and still the morning does not come.
(strained, sung low)
And still, I am eternal.

[Call and Response]
(female operatic lead, desperate)
Kyrie... have I not paid enough?
(low choir, gregorian, answering beneath)
Requiem... requiem aeternam...

[Build-Up]
[Layering]
(the choir thickens; the drum subdivides, quickening beneath a fixed pulse)
(lead, rising)
I hold what is left of me together
with the same cold hand that holds the dead.

[Crescendo]
(belted, rising into a banshee wail)
Let the wrath come — dies irae —
(wailing)
I have already ended, and I am still here.

[Breakdown]
(strip to near-silence, one whispered voice)
(whispered)
...dona eis requiem... dona eis...

[Chorus]
(chant overwhelms the lead — the mass swells and buries the one)
(choir, overwhelming)
Requiem. Requiem aeternam.
(lead, keening above the choir, a banshee cry that will not be buried)
...still, I am eternal...

[Outro]
(decrescendo — the last breath, air running out)
(whispered, fading)
...requiem... aeternam...
(instrumental: the bell once more, the drone, then nothing)
```

  lines **command the phrasing** (the hushed collapse on "one light") more than v1's single line did? If
  yes → **line-breaking is a real phrasing lever** (promote it to the recipe, alongside meta-tags-as-forecast).
- **Terminology** — do *fading* and *eternal* land with the grace intended, and does the "eternal" line
  keep its weight where "stop" had it?
