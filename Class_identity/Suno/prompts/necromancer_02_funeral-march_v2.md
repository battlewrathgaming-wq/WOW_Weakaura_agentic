## Lyrics (Write box) — v2, with the two banshee cues placed

```
[Intro]
(instrumental: a single deep funeral drum, a tolling bell, a low drone rising)

[Verse]
(whispered, spent, breath running thin)
Cold in the long dark. One light, and it is going.
I have paid. I have paid — and still the morning does not come.
(strained, sung low)
And still, I do not stop.

[Call and Response]
(female operatic lead, desperate)
Kyrie... have I not paid enough?
(low choir, gregorian, answering beneath)
Requiem... requiem aeternam...

[Build-Up]
[Layering]
(the choir thickens; the drum subdivides, quickening beneath a fixed pulse)
(lead, rising)
I hold what is left of me together
with the same cold hand that holds the dead.

[Crescendo]
(belted, rising into a banshee wail)
Let the wrath come — dies irae —
(wailing)
I have already ended, and I am still here.

[Breakdown]
(strip to near-silence, one whispered voice)
(whispered)
...dona eis requiem... dona eis...

[Chorus]
(chant overwhelms the lead — the mass swells and buries the one)
(choir, overwhelming)
Requiem. Requiem aeternam.
(lead, keening above the choir, a banshee cry that will not be buried)
...still, I do not stop...

[Outro]
(decrescendo — the last breath, air running out)
(whispered, fading)
...requiem... aeternam...
(instrumental: the bell once more, the drone, then nothing)
```

## What to confirm

- **Does the wail land at the two placed moments** — the `[Crescendo]` belt and the `[Chorus]` — and
  **only** there (not smeared across the track)? That's the test: *placed = callable*.
- If it lands on cue → banshee graduates to **callable** in [`summonable-features.md`](../summonable-features.md)
  (confidence: reconfirmed). If it doesn't → try a plainer cue word, or note it needs the emergent stack alone.
- Compare to v1: same track, but is the wail now **where you want it**, and is the rest unchanged?
