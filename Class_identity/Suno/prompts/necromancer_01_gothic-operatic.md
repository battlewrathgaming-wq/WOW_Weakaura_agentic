## Lyrics (Write box) — line structure carrying the cold march

```
[Intro]
(instrumental: deep war-drum ostinato, grand organ swelling, a distant tolling bell)

[Verse]
(low, cold, marching)
We do not kneel.
We do not warm.
We rose from the ash of a kingdom that burned.
(building)
And we answer one voice in the dark.

[Build-Up]
(the drum subdivides, the choir gathers beneath)
(rising)
Dark Lady —
your word is the march.
your will, the blade we became.

[Chorus]
(powerful operatic female lead, grand, driving)
In service of the dark.
In service of the Lady who would not die.
(low choir, answering)
Dark Lady, watch over us.
(lead, soaring)
We are the vengeance she kept.

[Breakdown]
(strip to organ and a single whisper)
(whispered)
she was murdered.
and she rose.
so did we all.

[Crescendo]
(belted, rising into a banshee wail)
Let the living fear the cold —
(wailing)
the Banshee Queen has called us home.

[Chorus]
(full — choir and lead together, the war-march at its peak)
In service of the dark.
In service of the Lady who would not die.
(wailing)
Dark Lady — we do not fall.

[Outro]
(the march receding — drum, organ, a last bell)
(low, cold, certain)
We do not kneel.
We do not die.
We serve.
```
