# Prompt — Necromancer × Form 06 (Intimate Hollow) · Driver B

_Form: [06 Intimate Hollow (Song for the Absent)](../forms/06_intimate-hollow.md) — **its first track**
(the form's first live test). Content **(Battlewrath): the Culling of Stratholme, from the Forsaken side** —
an intimate, **one-sided address to Arthas**, the beloved prince who became their slaughter. The cruelest
choice (cull the living so they can't rise, or let the Scourge grow); he chose the blade — and they rose
anyway, and he fell anyway. She sings *to* him, who cannot hear (the form's whole register). Driver B, sparse
piano, present close voice, **no banshee** (wrong for this intimacy). A first draft to run and grade._

## Lyrics (Write box) — sparse, close, one-sided; line structure for the tender delivery

```
[Intro]
(a single piano in a large empty room, long reverb, silence between the notes)

[Verse]
(close, tender, almost spoken — a personal message)
They loved you.
Do you remember that?
Before the snow. before the sword.
You were ours.

[Verse]
(quiet, aching)
And they gave you the cruelest choice a prince could hold —
cut us down where we stood,
or let us rise and drown the world.

[Chorus]
(still soft, swelling only barely — the voice stays close)
So you chose.
You chose us.
You walked the streets you were born to keep,
and you laid us down like grain.

[Verse]
(barely there — forgiving and broken at once)
I do not hate you.
I have tried. I cannot.
I only wish that someone had told you —

[Bridge]
(the smallest lift, then back to the hollow)
it did not matter.
We rose anyway.
And so, my prince... did you.

[Outro]
(the piano thinning to nothing — the room, then silence)
(whispered, close, to one who cannot hear)
Sleep now.
if you can.
I am still here. and I forgive the boy you were.
```

## What to confirm

- **The form (06's first live test)** — does the arrangement **create absence for the voice** (sparse
  piano, room, silence; nothing competing), and does the voice land as a **present personal message** —
  not a limp whisper?
- **The register dial (your corner)** — I wrote her *aching and almost-forgiving* (she forgives *the boy
  he was*, not the Lich King). Is that the right note for the Forsaken here — or should it be more
  unforgiving, colder, more purely grieving? Easy to redraw.
- **Lead instrument** — piano (Sóley's), or a soft cello / low organ to sit closer to her?
- **The one-sidedness** — does it feel like she's singing to someone who cannot hear (the form's soul)?
