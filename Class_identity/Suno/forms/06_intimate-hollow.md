# Song Form 06 — Intimate Hollow (Song for the Absent)

_A **song-form spec**: form, not content (see [`01_gothic-operatic.md`](01_gothic-operatic.md) for the
law). Battlewrath's taste as structure; content attaches. **Sourced, not invented** — from his reference
+ description, 2026-08-01. **⚠** = unconfirmed._

**The intimate / small pole — the opposite of the grand forms.** Where 01/04 are grand and 02 is
processional, 06 is **still, sparse, and close.** Not a crowd; one voice in an empty room.

## The governing law: the arrangement creates ABSENCE for the voice

The defining rule (Battlewrath): **the instruments create absence so the voice can be heard — they never
compete for attention.** Few elements, a lot of room; silence and echo are *structural*. Whatever is
present **recedes** to leave space for the voice. (The inverse of the grand forms, where the voice rides a
big arrangement — here the arrangement withdraws beneath it.)

## The job of a track in this form

An intimate, private song — **played for someone absent.** Battlewrath: *"like she's playing for someone
she wishes was sat next to her, listening — or playing for the comfort of who she sings for, even if they
cannot listen."* Tender, longing, aching. Comfort offered into absence.

## Register — intimate, tender, longing (present, not limp)

- Intimate · tender · longing · vulnerable · aching. A *private* performance, not a performed one.
- **Threads the early rule, doesn't break it:** this is NOT a *whole whispery slow track* (which
  Battlewrath dislikes). The voice is **present** — a personal message, close and direct — and the
  *hollow* does the haunting. Tenderness with presence, never a limp whisper.

## The voice — a personal message

Close, direct, confiding; first-person-intimate, as if to *one* person beside her. **The focal point** —
everything else defers to it. It leans in, quiet; it does not soar or belt (contrast 01/04's lead). **And
the timbre is a _creature's_, not a young singer's** — see Vocal features.

## Instrumentation — IN (spare)

A single lead instrument carrying it — ideally a **found / worn piano** (detuned, felt-muted, slightly out
of tune, faint mechanical hammer noise — *a forgotten upright in an empty room, not a pristine maintained
grand*; Battlewrath, 2026-08-01), or a soft cello / low organ · room reverb and echo (the hollowed tones)
· **silence and space as material** · perhaps one distant, sparse texture. Whatever is present stays
*under* the voice.

## Instrumentation & tone — OUT

- **No wall of sound; nothing that competes with the voice** (the governing law). No busy arrangement, no
  big build crowding it.
- No guitar wall / metal (as the core forms). Not grand, driving, or bombastic. **Not a march** (that's
  02). And not a limp whisper-drone — the voice must stay present.

## Dynamic / arc

Slow, still, sustained. **Restraint is the point** — minimal build; if it swells at all, only barely, and
it returns to the hollow. The absence is *held*, not filled.

## Sensory palette

Hollowed echo tones · cold but tender · spacious, sparse · room-sound and reverb-as-emptiness · a single
close voice in a large empty space.

## Vocal features

The **personal-message register** (close, confiding) is the whole voice here — but the **timbre is a
creature's, not a singer's** (Battlewrath, 2026-08-01): a **banshee speaking in her softest register** —
spectral, aged, weathered, cold, otherworldly — *not* young, clean, or perky. **Not the banshee _wail_**
(summonable #001's loud cry — wrong for this intimacy); this is the *same undead voice held soft*. The
softness is a monster's softness. (Pairs with *closeness* — a voice mic'd intimate, almost in the room.)
_Corrects the earlier draft, which wrongly waved the banshee off entirely — it's the wail that's out, not
the voice._

## Content resonance (a note — content attaches later, loosely)

*"Even if they cannot listen"* is achingly on-theme for the necromancer — singing tenderly to the dead, or
to a lost one who cannot hear. But that's **content** to attach; the form is the intimate register itself.
