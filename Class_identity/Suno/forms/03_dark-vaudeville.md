# Song Form 03 — Dark Vaudeville (Gallows Whimsy)

_A **song-form spec**: form, not content (see [`01_gothic-operatic.md`](01_gothic-operatic.md) for the
form-vs-content law). Battlewrath's taste as structure, necro-agnostic, reusable; content attaches onto
it. **Sourced, not invented** — from the 2026-07-30 taste conversation (form 03). **⚠** = unconfirmed._

**The whimsy corner.** In the set of forms: **01 drives the darkness, 02 grieves it, 03 laughs at it.**
The gallows-humor register of the same gothic world — the levity that keeps a playlist from being
unrelenting, and still sits *in* the space (the Forsaken's own morbid wit).

## The job of a track in this form

A grim grin. Whimsy and levity in the same world the other forms take deadly seriously — macabre played
for **fun**, not dread. The tonal inverse of 02: 02 sings the last breath; 03 makes a *joke* of it.

## The core shape (one line)

Dark cabaret / **steampunk vaudeville**: a wry macabre *persona* griping and singing over communal,
strongly-metered dance/work-song rhythms — **shanty roll and waltz lilt** — guitar-free, theatrical,
tongue firmly in cheek.

## Register — the whimsy, and its three edges (rotate per song)

Macabre-comic, theatrical, wry. The whimsy spans a spectrum, and — like 02's rotating focus — **each song
picks a point on it:**

- **Gallows humor** — genuinely morbid, laughing in death's face.
- **Absurd & silly** — light, daft, playful nonsense.
- **Sinister-playful** — mad-villain menace grinning under the fun (Dr. Steel).

All three belong; a given track commits to one. Grandiose but *winking* — never taking itself straight.

## Rhythm — communal, strongly metered, bouncing

- **Sea-shanty** — call-and-response, stomping work-song roll, gang/singalong vocals.
- **Waltz** — the 3/4 steampunk lilt (Ghostfire's *Last Steampunk Waltz*). Also oom-pah / music-hall.
- **Fixed signatures** (his wheelhouse) — but **lilting and danceable, not marching**. Bouncing where 01
  drove and 02 processed.

## Voice — the character on the stage

- **Character-voiced, first-person, theatrical / vaudeville.** A *persona* — griping, boasting, mad,
  put-upon — performing to a crowd.
- **★ Grievance / complaint humor** as the signature device: the narrator wryly complaining about a
  dark or absurd lot ("the zombie's perspective," "complaints of being an alchemist" — the content that
  rides this form).
- **Call-and-response chorus** — the communal shanty crowd answering the lead.

## Content that rides this form (examples)

The **wry macabre persona** griping about their existence — filed as **content**, attached onto the form:
- *The zombie's perspective.*
- *Complaints of being an alchemist.*

The form is the stage and the tone; these are the characters who walk onto it. (Form × content.)

## Instrumentation — IN

Accordion / concertina · fiddle · music-hall / honky-tonk piano · oom-pah brass (tuba) · stomps & claps ·
gang / group vocals · **steampunk clockwork textures** — cogs, gears, ticking, music-box, mechanical
clanks, steam-whistle · bells · fairground / calliope organ. _(Focus can rotate per song, per the
layer law borrowed from 02.)_

## Instrumentation & tone — OUT / boundaries

- **No guitar — it has its own forms (04 & 05).** The "no-guitar" line holds clean here; guitar energy is
  quarantined to the "going full" forms, not blended into the whimsy.
- **No heavy-metal wall** (that's 04/05 too).
- **"Never tinny" is _relaxed_ here (confirmed 2026-07-30).** Music-box, clockwork, and calliope tinkle
  are welcome — this is the **playful space**, and the brightness serves it. (The bass-anchored /
  not-tinny rule is 01/02's, not universal.)
- **Piano is welcome here (confirmed 2026-07-30)** — a music-hall / honky-tonk piano earns a real seat;
  the "piano loses richness" caution was about the *grand* forms, not the cabaret.

## Dynamic / arc

Theatrical **story-song**: verse → rousing communal chorus, comic timing, a singalong payoff. Likely
**tighter song-lengths** (not 10-minute epics). ⚠ Confirm length feel.

## Sonic character

Characterful, theatrical, mechanical — **brighter and more mid-forward** than 01/02 by nature of the
palette (accordion, fiddle, clockwork, music-box). The brightness is *welcome* here; the whimsy isn't
bound to the bass-anchored rule.

## The bridge to a prompt (light)

Form supplies the *style / production / structure* — the shanty/waltz meter, the cabaret palette, the
clockwork textures, the persona-voice and call-and-response, and **which whimsy-edge** the song commits
to. Content supplies the persona and their complaint. Form × content = a prompt.

## Generation caveats (targets for the prompt library)

- **The wry-comic delivery** — getting a *persona* that sounds knowing and tongue-in-cheek, not earnest.
- **Clockwork / music-box textures** — steampunk mechanical detail Suno may not reach for unprompted.
