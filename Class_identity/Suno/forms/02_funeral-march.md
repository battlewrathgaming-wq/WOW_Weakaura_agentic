# Song Form 02 — Funeral March (The Last Breath)

_A **song-form spec**: form, not content (see [`01_gothic-operatic.md`](01_gothic-operatic.md) for the
form-vs-content law). Battlewrath's taste as pure structure, necro-agnostic, reusable; content attaches
onto it. **Sourced, not invented** — distilled from the 2026-07-30 taste conversation (form 02). Items
marked **⚠** are unconfirmed by him — provisional, flagged loud._

**The deliberate complement of 01.** Form 01 was *avoidant of atmosphere* — driven, never ambient. Form
02 is **all about atmosphere** — but with the one condition atmosphere usually fails.

## The governing law: atmospheric, but never directionless

Most pure-instrumental atmosphere loses you because it *drifts*. This form refuses that. **Pace =
direction; layers = depth; every element does one or the other.** The direction comes from the
**funeral march** — a processional tread underneath, so the atmosphere layers *over forward motion*
instead of floating free. (This is 02's crux, the mirror of 01's "drive, never chaos.")

## The job of a track in this form

An atmosphere to **inhabit** while playing — a space to exist inside, not a groove to lock to. It holds
you by *processing forward*, a cortège you walk in, rather than by driving you. The slow, solemn
counterweight to 01.

## The core shape (one line)

A funeral cortège: a **slow drum tread**, each strike **prolonged by a tolling bell's tail**, under
**desperate, corrupted Gregorian chant** — atmosphere that marches instead of drifting.

## Pace — the tread (the direction)

- **Funeral drum — the primary driver.** Deep, slow, measured strikes: the step of the cortège. This is
  the spine and the direction.
- **Tolling bells ride the background**, **prolonging each drum strike** — the bell's long resonant tail
  carries the space between steps and hands off to the next. (The deep, **not-tinny** church bells from
  01 find their true home here.) Bells are pace *and* a layer at once.
- Slow, processional, solemn. (⚠ tempo band unpinned, but "funeral march" sets it slow and measured.)

## Layers — the atmosphere (the depth)

- The layer-stack can contain **all** the elements — organ drone, low strings, tolling bells, distant
  wind, breath, the chant doubled *under* itself — each **taking a different place** (near/far, high/low,
  fore/back).
- **★ Focus rotates per song.** Any layer can step forward as the **focus of a different track** while
  the rest recede — one song fronts the chant, another the organ, another lets the bells lead. **Shared
  palette, rotating foreground → variety *within* the form.** (May generalize to the whole lane; noted
  here where it was named.)

## Vocal core — pure Gregorian, corrupted by desperation

- **Pure Gregorian chant** — sacred, Latin, monophonic — as the voice core (central here, not a break).
- **★ The unholy twist is _desperation_.** Not harmonic dissonance, not a growl underneath — *expressive*
  corruption. Religious chant reaches **up**: angelic, transcendent, straining toward heaven. This
  reaches the other way — **the last breath of the lungs.** Not ascending to grace; *expiring*. Unholy
  because it has given up on heaven and sings the liturgy anyway. **Despair wearing the robes.**
- **Sung spent** (a performance direction): breathy at the edges, straining, air running out — each
  phrase a final exhalation trailing into nothing. **Spent, not serene.**
- Voice-as-instrument taken all the way: chant is *pure* cadence, sound over story (01's hard line, at
  its purest here).

## Lead × chant — two bodies in dialogue

02 carries **both a lead voice and the chant** — and their **interplay is a compositional engine**, a
second source of the direction this form demands. Four modes, moved between:

- **In concert** — the two together, unison or harmony, one body.
- **In the other's absence** — each worked against the *space the other leaves*: the lead sings into the
  chant's silence, or the chant fills where the lead has gone. Absence used as a voice.
- **★ Lead dispels the chant** — the single voice rises and *scatters* the mass, banishing it.
- **★ Chant overwhelms the lead** — the mass swells and *drowns* the individual voice beneath it.

The one and the many, contending — and that contention **carries the piece forward** (part of how an
atmospheric form stays directional).

> **Discovered timbre (2026-07-30):** the lead can carry a **banshee wail / keening** — a piercing
> spectral cry, loved on the first generation (*"Forsaken in requiem aeternam"*). Now a **summonable
> feature** ([`../summonable-features.md`](../summonable-features.md) #001) — invoked by **placed inline
> cues** (`(wailing)` / `(keening)` on chosen lead lines), *not* a Styles tag (that would over-use it). A
> punctuation, not wallpaper. On-theme — a banshee is a wailing undead spirit.

## Register

Funereal, solemn, grave, **spent, desperate.** Sacred-corrupted. The atmosphere of the last breath.
(The "soft, desperate moments" that were a *contrast* in 01 are the **whole spine** of 02.)

## Instrumentation — IN

Funeral drum · tolling church bells (not tinny) · organ (drone / layer) · low strings · desperate
Gregorian chant · distant wind & breath. _(Focus rotates among these per song, per the layer law.)_

## Instrumentation & tone — OUT (hard lines)

- **Inherited from your overall taste (⚠ confirm they carry to this form):** no guitar · no synth-focus ·
  never tinny · bass-rich over thin.
- **Form-specific:** not **serene / angelic** (the desperation forbids it) · not **directionless** (the
  march forbids it).

## Dynamic / arc

Direction has **two engines** now: the **funeral-march tread** (pace) and the **lead × chant contention**
(the vocal drama above). ⚠ Still open: whether a track *also* **builds by accreting layers** toward a
peak (the cortège swelling as it proceeds) or **holds a solemn plateau** while the voices carry the
motion. Likely a per-song choice — to settle with Battlewrath.

## Sonic character

Deep, bass-rich, **resonant** — bell tails, drum body, cavernous space. Spent breath at the top instead
of a bright sheen. The room is large and cold.

## The bridge to a prompt (light)

This form supplies a Suno prompt's *style / production / structure* half — the drum tread, the bell
tails, the desperate-chant texture, the layer stack, and **which element is in focus**. Content supplies
the theme / any text, attached from an identity. Form × content = a prompt. _(Content layer not built yet.)_

## Generation caveats (targets for the prompt library)

- **Tolling church bells** — hard to generate cleanly (carried from 01); here they *toll*, a fresh angle
  to crack.
- **Chant that sounds _spent_, not serene** — Suno will default Gregorian chant to the angelic. Making it
  the *last breath* — breathy, straining, expiring — is the craft target of this whole form.
