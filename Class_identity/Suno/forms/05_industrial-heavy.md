# Song Form 05 — Industrial Heavy (Cold Machine)

_A **song-form spec**: form, not content (see [`01_gothic-operatic.md`](01_gothic-operatic.md) for the
law). Drawn from Battlewrath's reference cluster and **graded by him 2026-07-30** — the opens below
resolved, a clear pass. His taste, as form._

**The _cold_ half of "going full."** Split from 04 (the grand/melodic half). Where 04 is catharsis
through grandeur, **05 is catharsis through force** — hard, mechanical, menacing, horror-tinged.

## The core shape (one line)

**Cold, heavy, machine-driven groove** — downtuned and pounding, martial and precise, with a harsh
voice barking over the top. Menace, not grandeur.

## Register

**Cold · hard · mechanical · martial · aggressive · dirty.** Machine-heaviness (Rammstein) and
horror-groove (Rob Zombie) — menace with a camp/horror grin at the edges. The merciless face of the dark,
where 04 is its grand face and 02 its grieving one.

## Voice

- **★ Harsh — the one new vocal color in the set.** Deep guttural (Rammstein), snarl / growl (Rob
  Zombie), barked and chanted-hard. Percussive, martial, front-of-teeth. None of 01–04 go here.
- A **clean voice may cut through** on a given song (a hook, a chant), but the **harsh bark is home** —
  05's identity is the guttural (confirmed; a per-song choice).

## Rhythm & drive

**Heavy locked groove** — mechanical precision, martial stomp, downtuned riffing. Extremely **fixed**
(Rammstein is metronomic): this is **drive-not-chaos at its most machine-like** — the fixed floor turned
to iron. Groove over flurry.

## Instrumentation — IN

Downtuned heavy **guitars** · **machine / industrial percussion** + martial drums · deep bass · horror
samples & textures (Rob Zombie) · hard-barked vocals.

## The boundary that bends here

- **Synth / electronic is _relaxed_ for 05 (confirmed 2026-07-30).** Industrial synth, samples, and
  machine textures are **welcome** — 05 bends "no-synth" the way 03 bent "no-tinny" (each form bends a
  different rule). Kept **under the guitars and the groove**, serving the machine-heaviness — *not*
  synth-pop up front.

## Relationship to the set

- **05 vs 04** — 04 grand / melodic / warm; **05 cold / hard / dirty / mechanical.** The two faces of
  "going full."
- **05 vs 01** — both driven and fixed; 01 is organic and restrained, 05 is electric, downtuned, and
  merciless. Same spine (the locked floor), opposite skin.

## Generation caveats (targets for the prompt library)

- **Harsh vocals that stay _controlled_** (drive, not chaos) — barked/guttural without becoming noise.
- **Machine precision** — the metronomic, mechanical lock that makes Rammstein *drive*.
