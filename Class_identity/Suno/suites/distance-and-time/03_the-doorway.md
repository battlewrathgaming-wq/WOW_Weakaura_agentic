## Write box — instrumental directives (drive the arrangement; no sung words)

```
[Instrumental. At the threshold — the doorway. 80% grand organ and cello, close and dry now. The one event: deep shuddering knocks on a great door. Gothic. Wordless textures only, NO sung words. Directions only.]

[Intro]
(the grand organ and deep cello, close and present now, the reverb tightened to a room; poised, waiting at the door)

[Knock]
(three deep, shuddering knocks land on a great wooden door — heavy, ominous, reverberating through the space; the first real transient of the suite; organ and cello hold beneath, breath-held)

[Build]
(organ and cello swell, tense and expectant — da — da — daaaah — the knocks echo and fade; something is about to open)

[Peak]
(a final, heavier shuddering knock — a deep bell strikes with it; organ and cello hold the open note, poised on the threshold)

[Outro]
(the knocks settle, the organ and cello hold — the door about to give; at the threshold, not yet inside)
```

## What to confirm

- **The knocks** — do the **deep, shuddering door-knocks** land as the suite's *one* transient — heavy,
  ominous, reverberant — and did dropping "percussion" from Exclude let them through (without a drum kit
  sneaking in)?
- **Closer / drier than track 2** — reverb tightened toward a room, organ + cello close and present; the
  distance-atmosphere (banshee, crows) gone — you're *at the door*, not still approaching.
- **Still 80% organ + cello** — the knocks are the event, but the organ and cello carry it.
- **Poised, not yet inside** — it should feel like the threshold, setting up track 4's *interior*.
